# Cosmology & The Planet

Enphera is the third planet of the pale yellow star Vaelis, orbiting at a comfortable distance that sustains liquid water across most of its surface. A single natural companion, the cratered moon Theris, governs the tides and punctuates the night sky. The solar year is divided into **364 days**, grouped into thirteen months of exactly twenty-eight days each, with each month composed of four seven-day weeks.

The planet's axial tilt is mild — roughly nineteen degrees — producing four distinct but temperate seasons across most latitudes. Equatorial regions experience wet and dry seasons rather than the four-season cycle familiar to inhabitants of the mid-latitudes. The atmosphere is slightly richer in oxygen than Earth's, supporting the large-bodied fauna that roam the wilder continents.

## The Sky Above

By day the sky carries the familiar cyan-blue tint; at dusk it shades into deep amber and finally a violet-purple before true night. Two faint planetary neighbors, occasionally visible to the naked eye, were named **Lir Andar** ("the Wanderer") and **Lir Vesh** ("the Quiet One") by the earliest astronomers of Vona. Their true nature as sister worlds was only established in the last two centuries.

## The Moon Theris

Theris is tidally locked, presenting the same face to Enphera at all times. Dark basalt plains called the **Quiet Hemisphere** face the planet; the far side is rugged and bright. Lunar eclipses occur roughly twice a year and are treated as omens in many folk traditions.
