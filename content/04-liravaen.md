# The Liravaen Language

Liravaen is the principal language of the Liravaen people and one of the oldest continuously written languages on Enphera. It is polysynthetic, head-marking, and exhibits ergative-absolutive alignment. Despite its complexity, it has remained remarkably stable over the past two millennia thanks to a strong literary canon maintained by the **Order of the Long Memory**.

## Phonology

Liravaen has twenty-two consonant phonemes and eight vowels. Phonation includes a contrast between modal and breathy voice, which is marked orthographically with a trailing *h*.

## Grammar Notes

- Word order is free but **verb-final** is the unmarked pattern in formal prose.
- Possession is marked on the possessed noun, not the possessor.
- Numerals above seven are borrowed from Vona Trade, an artefact of historical trade contact.
