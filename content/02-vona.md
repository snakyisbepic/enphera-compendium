# The Continent of Vona

Vona is the northwestern-most of Enphera's four major landmasses, spanning roughly nine million square kilometres from the subarctic shores of the Korval Sea to the subtropical Verga peninsula. Its human inhabitants — collectively called the **Vonan peoples** — share a deep cultural memory of the Long Migration, a multi-century diaspora from the eastern interior to the coastlines that reshaped the continent's politics and language families.

> The wind that crosses the Verga passes every door before it reaches the sea. — Vonan proverb

## Geography

Vona is bounded on the west by the Amber Ocean, on the north by the Korval Sea, and on the east by the Voral Range — a high mountain chain whose peaks remain snow-capped year-round. The interior is dominated by the **Valen Plateau**, a dry steppe that has historically supported horse-lord cultures and now forms the breadbasket of the southern confederations.

### Major Regions

1. **The Verga Peninsula** — Mediterranean climate; olive groves, vineyards, and the great trading city of Sol Vir.
2. **The Valen Plateau** — Temperate grassland; grain agriculture, horse-breeding, and the old fortress-cities of Kor and Marand.
3. **The Voral Range** — Mining, alpine pastoralism, and isolated monastic communities.
4. **The Northern Reach** — Boreal forest and tundra; home to the Kenar peoples.

## Languages

The Vonan language family is divided into three principal branches:

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Plateau | Marandi | Cursive Kor | ~14 million |

## History at a Glance

- **Era of the Long Migration** (c. -1200 to -800) — Population movements from east to west.
- **First Vergan League** (c. -340 to +120) — Maritime trading confederation.
- **The Compact of Kor** (year 412) — Unification of the plateau cities.
- **The Modern Confederation** (year 1843 onward) — Present political arrangement.
