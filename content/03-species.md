# Species of Enphera

Enphera supports a diverse biosphere with several sapient and near-sapient species, alongside a bestiary of notable fauna. This chapter is currently under revision as new fieldwork is integrated.

## Sapient Peoples

| Species | Avg. Height | Lifespan | Notable Trait |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Humans (Vonan) | 1.7 m | 80 yr | Adaptive, short generational cycle |
| Korathi | 2.1 m | 140 yr | Slight subdermal ossification; bone-white skin |
| Therin | 1.4 m | 60 yr | Echolocation; large dark-adapted eyes |
| Liravaen | 1.9 m | 320 yr | Photographic memory; bio-luminescent markings |

> The Korathi do not bury their dead; they *return* them. Every stone in a Korathi wall is, in some sense, a monument. — Therin observer, year 1771

## The Liravaen

The Liravaen are the longest-lived of the sapient species and the keepers of the oldest continuous literary tradition on the planet. Their language, also called **Liravaen**, is polysynthetic with an unusually large phoneme inventory; a single word can encode what would require an entire sentence in Vona Trade.

### Sample Liravaen Vocabulary

| Liravaen | Literal Gloss | Common Translation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *val-thar-en* | "the one who walks before dawn" | scout; forerunner |
| *kor-sel* | "bone-of-the-mountain" | marble |
| *mira-vo-lir* | "we-remember-the-river" | our shared ancestry |

## Bestiary Highlights

- **Voral Hound** — Large pack predator of the eastern slopes.
- **Vergan Sand-Lizard** — Domesticated for eggs and leather.
- **Therin Cave-Moth** — Symbiotic with Therin settlements; reflects echolocation.
